# Incident Report

**Reference:** AV-0614 **Reporting officer:** DS P. Nandakumar
**Location:** Victoria Glasshouse, Fennimore Botanical Trust, Ashcombe Vale
**Date of incident:** Friday 14 June

## Summary

At **18:40** the head groundskeeper, Mr Abe Lindqvist, entered the glasshouse with his own keypad code and found Dr Odile Marchetti (58) collapsed between beds 4 and 5. He called an ambulance at 18:41. Dr Marchetti was taken to Ashcombe General and died at 23:15.

## Scene

- The single door was **locked**. It locks automatically when closed and opens from outside with a four-digit code. From inside, it opens with a push bar.
- All **roof vents were closed**. The temperature was 31 °C.
- A steel **flask** (item A) was on the potting bench, about a third full of herbal tea.
- A **Thornguard** canister (item B) was on the potting bench with its cap off.
- No sign of forced entry.

## Door code

The shared door code, supplied by Director Hollis Crane, is **2906**. The grounds team uses a separate code.

## Fingerprints

Prints on the outer door handle were matched on the database to **Felix Marchetti**, the victim's nephew (prints on file from a previous caution). Mr Marchetti was traced on Saturday. A partial print on the underside of the flask lid has been **sent to the lab** and is not yet processed.

## Initial assessment

The victim was probably spraying Thornguard with the vents shut and was overcome by fumes. **Recommend: accidental death.** Awaiting toxicology.
