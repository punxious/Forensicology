## Wednesday 12th

H. pushing hard on the meadow again. She can bully the committee, but she can't bully me. The objection is in.

## Thursday 13th

F. came by, asking for money for the third time this year. I said no and he sulked off. He'll get the cottage one day anyway; I wish he'd just be patient and kind.

Something far worse. I measured the fern trays myself. The growth figures in our paper are **invented**. Every "drought-tolerant" result. W. wrote them all.

## Friday 14th, morning

Told W. I'm calling the editor at *Botanical Letters* on Monday to withdraw the paper and report the data. She cried, then went very quiet. She asked me to wait a month. I won't. Thirty years of honest work. I won't put my name to a lie.

*She's been very attentive today. Made a point of telling me she'd topped up my tea.*
