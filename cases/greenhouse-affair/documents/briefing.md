**Ashcombe Vale Forensic Science Unit**
**To:** Duty Examiner
**From:** DS Priya Nandakumar, Ashcombe Vale Police
**Re:** AV-0614, Dr Odile Marchetti

At 18:40 on Friday, Dr Odile Marchetti, Head of Glasshouse Collections at the Fennimore Botanical Trust, was found collapsed inside the Victoria Glasshouse. The door was locked and the roof vents were shut. She died in hospital later that night.

An uncapped canister of Thornguard, an organophosphate pesticide, was on the potting bench. My inspector wants this closed as an accidental exposure before the coroner's call. **That call starts in sixty minutes.**

I'm not convinced. Dr Marchetti had thirty years in glasshouses and was famously careful. Please go through the case file and tell me three things before the call:

1. **Who** did this, if anyone did
2. **Why** they did it
3. **How** they did it

The evidence bags are on their way to your bench. Some items are still locked or awaiting lab work; you'll get them as you make progress.

*— P.N.*
