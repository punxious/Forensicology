# Toxicology Report

**Case:** AV-0614 · **Subject:** O. Marchetti · **Examiner:** Duty Examiner, FSU

| Sample | Test | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Post-mortem blood | Cholinesterase activity | **Normal.** No organophosphate exposure. |
| Post-mortem blood | Cardiac glycosides | **Oleandrin**, at a toxic level |
| Flask residue (item A) | Cardiac glycosides | **Oleandrin**, at a high concentration |
| Flask residue (item A) | Organophosphates | Not detected |
| Canister (item B) | Organophosphates | Thornguard confirmed. Nozzle dry: **not recently used.** |

## Opinion

Death was caused by **oleandrin poisoning**. Oleandrin is the heart poison found in *Nerium oleander*. It was taken by mouth, in the tea in her flask. The symptoms (nausea, a slow and irregular heartbeat) match.

The digitalis glycosides found in foxglove were tested for and **not** found. The Thornguard canister had not been sprayed. It was probably placed on the bench to suggest an accident.
