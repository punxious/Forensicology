# What Really Happened

Wren Talbot was arrested on Saturday evening. Faced with the print on the flask lid and the bag of oleander leaves from her locker, she made a full confession.

**Motive.** The drought-tolerance data in her joint paper with Dr Marchetti was invented. When Odile found out and promised to report it on Monday, Wren's PhD, her funding and her career were all at stake.

**Preparation.** On Wednesday she pruned the oleander in bed 7 and kept the cuttings instead of composting them. Abe noticed they were missing. At home she steeped them into a concentrated extract.

**The afternoon.** At 16:58 Odile phoned to ask her to come back and talk. Wren locked her library terminal, badged out at 17:02 and let herself into the glasshouse with the shared code at 17:07. The keypad clock showed 16:07. While Odile talked, Wren emptied the dropper into her flask of lemon verbena tea, screwing the lid back on with a bare hand. On her way out at 17:16 she took the Thornguard canister from the store and left it uncapped on the bench. She shut the vents so it would look like fumes.

**Red herrings.** Felix lied about not seeing his aunt because Thursday's visit ended in an argument about money. His prints on the door handle were a day old. Hollis Crane wanted Odile out of the way of the meadow sale, but she spent the whole afternoon in front of a planning committee.

The Thornguard was meant to make a careful botanist look careless. The evidence made her look careful to the end: she wrote down her suspicions, locked them away, and left you the key.

*Dr Odile Marchetti's objection to the East Meadow sale was upheld in July. The Fennimore fern paper was withdrawn.*
